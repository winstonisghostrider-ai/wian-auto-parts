## Brand / origin copy

- Company history: https://www.bmcairfilters.com/en/about-us
- Motor Valley location and racing-to-aftermarket context: https://www.bmcairfilters.com/en

The About Us timeline states that BMC began in 1973 through Gaetano Bergami's
motorcycling passion, began Ferrari Formula 1 cooperation in 1995, transferred
Formula 1 know-how into replacement-filter production in 1996, and maintained
production in Italy. The homepage identifies its base as Medicina, Bologna, in
the heart of Motor Valley. The hero makes no power or fuel-economy claims.
