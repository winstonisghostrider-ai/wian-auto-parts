QUESTIONS

Good to know

How much does a home service cost?

It depends on your car and the job, so we quote every request individually on WhatsApp after checking your registration. The quote shows parts and labour separately, and nothing starts until you approve it.

Which parts do you use?

Genuine, OEM or quality aftermarket parts, matched to your exact vehicle, such as WIX and MANN filters and Liqui Moly, Würth and Hella fluids. We tell you the brand and part number in the quote, and parts carry the manufacturer's warranty.

What if the mechanic finds something else?

We tell you what was found, with photos where useful, and quote it separately. Extra work is only done if you approve it.

What if a part is wrong or a job needs redoing?

If the part was the problem, we arrange the correct part and the job continues as soon as it arrives. If the workmanship was the problem, it is put right by the workshop or mechanic, and we give you a clear time for when it will be done. Either way, we keep you updated on WhatsApp.

Do you service company or rental fleets?

Yes. We can schedule regular servicing at your yard and keep a job record for each vehicle. Message us about your fleet.

Are you a mechanic? Join the WIAN network.

We work with independent and mobile mechanics across South Goa. WIAN finds the customers, sources the parts and handles the quotes. Message us to join.
