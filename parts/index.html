WHY IT MATTERS

A clogged cabin filter is the cheapest AC problem to fix

Weak AC airflow

A blocked filter restricts air through the blower, so the cabin cools slowly even when the AC system is healthy.

Musty smell and dust

Trapped dust, pollen and moisture breed odour. Goa's monsoon humidity makes this worse.

When to change

Most manufacturers list it at every service or yearly; dusty or humid use often needs it sooner. Check your service schedule, or ask us.
