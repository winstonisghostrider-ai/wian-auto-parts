Enquiries, quotes and prices

  • Prices shown on this website are the manufacturer's or supplier's listed MRP and are for reference. Your final price, stock and delivery time are confirmed in a quote on WhatsApp or by email.
  • Quotes show parts and labour separately. A call-out or travel charge for home service may apply to some areas, and if it does it is shown in the quote.
  • Labour for home service and workshop jobs is agreed with the partner mechanic or workshop for each enquiry, so it is quoted per job.
  • A quote is valid for the period it states. Supplier prices can change, so if no period is stated we will reconfirm the price before ordering if more than 7 days have passed.
  • No part is ordered and no work is started until you approve the quote.

Fitment and vehicle details

  • To match parts to your vehicle we ask for the registration number, VIN or make, model, engine and year. Please make sure the details you give us are correct.
  • We check part numbers before supplying, but some vehicles have mid-year changes or non-standard parts. Where fitment cannot be fully confirmed we will tell you before ordering.
  • Applications listed on this website come from manufacturer catalogues and are a guide only; we always confirm against your vehicle.

Orders, delivery and collection

  • Parts can be collected from WIAN in Margao or delivered within South Goa. Any delivery charge is shown in the quote.
  • Items not in stock are ordered from our suppliers; delivery times are estimates and depend on the supplier.
  • Ownership of goods passes to you once payment is received in full.

Returns and exchanges

  • Wrong part supplied by WIAN: if we supplied a part that does not match the vehicle details you gave us, we will exchange it or refund you.
  • Faulty parts: handled under the warranty section below.
  • Other returns: unused, unfitted items in original, undamaged packaging can be returned within 7 days of purchase, subject to the supplier accepting the return. A refund is made once the supplier credits us.
  • Not returnable: opened fluids, oils and chemicals; parts that have been fitted; electrical and electronic parts once installed; and parts ordered specially or imported for your vehicle, unless they are faulty or we supplied the wrong part.

Warranty

  • Parts carry the warranty given by their manufacturer. We will handle the claim with the supplier on your behalf.
  • Warranty does not cover normal wear, incorrect fitment by someone other than WIAN or our partners, accident damage, misuse, or use of incorrect fluids.
  • For a warranty claim we may need the part back, the invoice and details of the fault. The manufacturer decides the outcome of the claim.
  • Workmanship on home service and workshop jobs is the responsibility of the mechanic or workshop who carried out the work, as set out in the next sections. WIAN coordinates any rectification.

Home service and workshop jobs

  • Home service and workshop jobs are carried out by independent partner mechanics and workshops. WIAN identifies your vehicle, sources the parts, agrees the quote with you, books the job and keeps you updated.
  • For home service the vehicle must be parked somewhere the mechanic can work safely and legally, with reasonable access, for example a driveway, compound or building parking. We may need to reschedule if the site is unsafe or the weather makes the job impossible.
  • Some jobs cannot be done at home, such as clutch and gearbox work, major engine repairs, accident repairs and anything needing a lift. These are arranged at a partner workshop.
  • If the mechanic finds another problem during the job, we will tell you, with photos where useful, and quote it separately. Extra work is only done if you approve it.
  • If you supply your own parts, the mechanic will fit them as agreed, but WIAN cannot give a warranty on those parts or on problems they cause.
  • Please remove valuables from the vehicle before the job.

If a part is wrong or a job needs redoing

  • Part problem: if a part turns out to be wrong or faulty, whether supplied by WIAN or by you, we will arrange the correct part and the job will resume once it arrives. The mechanic or workshop waits for the part. If WIAN supplied the part, the replacement is at our cost. If you supplied the part, the replacement part is charged to you.
  • Workmanship problem: if the mechanic or workshop made a mistake, they will put it right. We will tell you clearly what happened and give you an expected completion time (ETA), and keep you updated until it is fixed.
  • Please tell us as soon as you notice a problem after a job, and avoid driving the vehicle if it is unsafe to do so.

Diagnostics, coding and performance

  • Diagnosis is a paid service. The diagnosis fee is confirmed before booking. A diagnosis gives the most likely cause based on fault codes, live data and testing; some faults need further investigation or test parts.
  • VCDS coding and adaptations are carried out at the vehicle owner's request on compatible Volkswagen Group vehicles. Some changes can affect manufacturer warranty or require the original settings to be restored before a dealer visit; we will explain this before coding.
  • Remapping and performance work is carried out with recognised tuning partners. Results depend on the vehicle's condition and any figures given are indicative. Telling your insurer about modifications is the owner's responsibility.
  • We do not carry out modifications that would make a vehicle illegal to use on public roads.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • If parts were ordered specially for your job and cannot be returned to the supplier, we may charge for those parts if you cancel.
  • If a mechanic cannot attend, we will contact you to agree a new slot.
